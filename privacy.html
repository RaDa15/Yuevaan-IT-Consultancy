Privacy

Last updated 6 August 2026

What we collect

Only what you type into the enquiry form: your name, organisation, email, phone, the service you are interested in, your timeline, and your message. Nothing else is captured.

Why we collect it

To reply to your enquiry and, if we work together, to scope the engagement. We do not sell it and we do not share it with third parties.

Cookies and tracking

This site sets no cookies and runs no advertising or tracking pixels, which is why you were not asked to dismiss a consent banner. The office map is embedded from Google.

Fonts and other third parties

Typefaces are currently served by Google Fonts, which means your browser requests them from Google when the page loads. We are moving these to our own server.

How long we keep it, and your access to it

Enquiries are kept for 24 months. Write to info@yuevaanconsultancy.bt to ask for a copy of what we hold about you, to correct it, or to have it deleted.
