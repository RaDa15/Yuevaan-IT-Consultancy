How we work

Scoped, built, then supported

Procurement here rewards teams that stay after handover. Ours does.

01 — Discover

Two weeks on your process

02 — Build

Fortnightly, in the open

Working software every two weeks on a staging environment you can log into, so surprises surface early.

Handover with a team behind it

Documentation, training for your staff, and an in-country support line — not a ticket queue in another timezone.
